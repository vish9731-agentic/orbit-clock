## Pause media while an alarm rings

RingingService sends KEYCODE_MEDIA_PAUSE (never PLAY_PAUSE) and requests AUDIOFOCUS_GAIN_TRANSIENT_EXCLUSIVE with USAGE_ALARM after entering the foreground. Successful or pending focus requests are shared across overlapping alarms, including silent/vibration-only alarms; a new alarm may retry a failed or permanently lost request. The last occurrence ending through Snooze, Stop, timeout or service destruction releases focus; Orbit never sends a Play command. A media app may resume on focus gain according to its own policy. A DELAYED focus request waits for gain (for example during a call), and subsequent focus loss stops the tone until gain.
